Christopher A. Rotunno is an analytics engineer based in St. Augustine, FL, working to bring super intelligence to the working class.

He's worked both sides of the dashboard: inside financial and operational data teams, and on the floor in retail, food service, and home improvement sales. The same lesson kept showing up: people don't fail, systems do. And the tools that could help the most rarely reach the people doing the work.

His writing translates super intelligence into plain English, with notebooks anyone can run, and his daily Tidbit of the Day on LinkedIn shares lessons for careers that don't run in a straight line.
