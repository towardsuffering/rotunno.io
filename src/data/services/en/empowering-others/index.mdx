- Giving people real ownership of their piece of the work, so they grow into the next level of responsibility instead of waiting for permission.
- Clearing obstacles instead of dictating steps, so people can solve problems the way they actually know how.
- Leading by example, setting a standard people want to rise to instead of one they're told to meet.
- Building short feedback loops, so people learn fast and keep improving instead of repeating the same mistake.
